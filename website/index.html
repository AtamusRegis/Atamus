Updates

Oct 17, 2026
  • The Shop. A new station window to buy modules and hulls, with what each one does.
  • Upgrades. Every module can be upgraded, and bigger hulls fit more modules at once.
  • Ships show their modules. Each module you fit adds its own section to your ship.
  • Cargo you can see. Your hold and station storage now show every unit, and you can check what it's worth and who helped mine it.
  • Claiming ore. Broken asteroids leave their ore beside them for anyone to claim.
  • New ore names. Ores are now named Alpha through Epsilon, rarer down the alphabet.
  • Ship menus and trading. Right-click a ship to invite, whisper, fuel or refine it, or to trade your cargo to a hauler.
  • All sectors open. The whole ring system is open all the time.
  • Party and corporation invites. Invites now pop up to accept or decline, and leaders and owners can remove members.
  • Friends on the minimap. Your party and corporation show on the minimap wherever they are.
  • Field refining. Refinery ships now offer to refine the cargo of everyone nearby.
  • Tutorial. New pilots get a short guided tour of mining, delivering, refining, selling and upgrading.
Oct 16, 2026
  • Saturn's rings. A new 2D world: fly out from the station around Saturn to mine the asteroids in its rings, with richer ores deeper out.
  • Mining together. Everyone who helps break an asteroid gets their name on the ore, and every named player gets a copy when it's delivered.
  • Modules. Fit your ship as a miner, hauler, fueler, tractor, command ship, refinery or scanner.
  • The station. Sell ore, refine it, turn ice into fuel, refuel and buy modules.
  • Fuel. Ships run on fuel, and fuelers can top up anyone who runs dry. Stranded ships can call for help.
  • Parties and corporations. Team up with other players, with their own chat channels.
  • Shards. Crowded zones split into copies so there's room for everyone, and parties stay together.
