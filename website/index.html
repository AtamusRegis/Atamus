Updates

Oct 14, 2026
  • Materializing ships. Destroyed ships now return as holograms that materialize before rejoining the fight.
  • Railguns. Ships can now fire glowing railgun slugs at each other, and walls and structures stop them.
  • Nebula clouds. Colorful nebula clouds now drift across the battlefield. Ships can hide inside them, their thick cores block the view to the other side, and their edges stir as ships pass through.
  • Ramming. Ships now shove each other when they collide, throwing out sparks, and hard hits cause damage, shown by health bars over damaged ships.
  • A sky of stars. The battlefield now floats over a deep blue nebula full of twinkling stars.
  • Line of sight. If you can see a ship, it can see you: ships behind walls and structures fade from view, and whatever you can't see falls into shadow.
  • New flight controls. Ships now fly the way they face, with smoother, weightier handling that lets them drift through turns.
Oct 13, 2026
  • Ship handling. Ships have weight and momentum, and a capacitor that powers sprinting.
  • Sides and a minimap. Choose Red or Blue when entering, and see friendlies and enemies on the minimap.
  • Chat. World and faction chat, private whispers, and reporting players.
  • Collisions. Ships bump into each other and into structures, and your aim line shows when something is in the way.
  • Hotbar and inventory. A hotbar and an inventory to arrange your items.
Oct 12, 2026
  • Atamus is now 3D. Rebuilt from the ground up as a persistent space warfare game in one shared 3D world, where you fly a cruiser.
