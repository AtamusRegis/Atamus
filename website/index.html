The Expanse

Atamus is a multiplayer space mining game you play right in your browser.

Start with a single mining frigate and a station in your own corner of the Expanse. Lock onto asteroids, cut ore, sell it on the market, and train your pilots in new licenses.

Buy bigger hulls, fit them with lasers and modules, and grow from one ship into a mining fleet. Push your ships past their limits when it counts.

Updates

Oct 11, 2026
  • Mute, block and support. Mute or block other players, and open support tickets to reach the admins.
  • Chat filter. Offensive words in chat are now hidden.
  • Celestials. Added the sun, planets and moons to the Expanse, with asteroid belts gathering around them.
  • Player stations. Every player now has their own station to dock at, store items in and refit their ships.
  • Drone control and reordering. Recall and redirect mining drones, watch them deliver ore to your ship, and drag items, training and targets into the order you want.
  • Range rings and chat bubbles. See your targeting and module ranges around your ship, and what nearby pilots say above their ships.
  • Drone hauling. Mining drones now fly their ore back to your ship.
  • Orbit and module power. Orbit objects in space, power modules off, and set mining modules on standby until a target is locked.
  • Logging off in space. Ships left in space keep working for a while, then warp to safety.
  • Modules and mining. Added mining lasers, mining drones, an afterburner and upgrades to fit to your ship, and mining asteroids for ore.
  • Station. Added a station to dock at and store your ore and modules in its hangar.
  • Mining Frigate license. Train the Mining Frigate license to strengthen your Prospector's hull bonuses.
  • Ship names and Look At. Rename your ships, and keep the camera on any object in space.
  • Ship fitting and pilot info. Added the Fitting window with your ship's hull, stats and requirements, hull info for ships, and Pilot Info for other players' pilots.
  • Flight. Ships now fly straight over asteroids and each other on the way to their destination.
  • The Library. Added the Library, a place to see every ship, structure and asteroid in the game.
  • Targeting. Lock asteroids and ships, align to them, fly to them and look them up.
  • Ship HUD. Added a hotbar with your ship's shields, hull and speed, your locked targets, and the ship inventory.
  • Direct messages and reporting. Message other players directly, look up their player sheet, or report them from chat.
  • Asteroid belts and warp. Added asteroid belts holding all five ores, and warp drives to reach them.
Oct 10, 2026
  • Back to basics. We've taken Atamus back to the start to get the core of flying and mining right. For now it's you, your pilots' Prospectors and the open Expanse. Everything else will return as it's rebuilt. Pilots, ships and credits were reset.
  • Characters and portraits. Create your character and your pilots with the new portrait creator. Your character is who you are in Local and Corp chat.
  • Fleet. Switch between your pilots from the Fleet bar or with Tab.
