Updates

Oct 18, 2026
  • Ore rides the beam. Ore bits now travel back down your laser straight into your hold.
  • Corium. Every core holds a new ore, corium, with more in bigger and deeper asteroids, bursting out when the core breaks.
  • New asteroids. Rocks are rebuilt layer by layer from the new chunk designs, with three rock densities and bright ore bits.
  • New ship art. Every ship has a fresh pixel-art look, and the Scanner, Booster and Hauler are back to fly.
  • Core piñatas. Mining is back to its heart: cut through the masonry, feed the core, and break it open to scatter every bit of ore for anyone to scoop up.
  • Name and play. Accounts are gone: type a pilot name and fly.
  • The beam runs hot. A held laser climbs from blue through yellow to red — cold beams cut clean, hot ones cut faster but cook the rock.
  • The charge shot. Miners can hold a chunk at its breaking point and release it as a blast that drives deep into the rock.
  • The Booster, the coordinator. It carries no laser now: a held beam boosts one ship at a time — green pluses and a blue glow — and a coolant spray cools overworked rock and cores.
  • Haulers refine in the field. A refine beam compresses another ship's cargo where they sit, and the Hauler's own hold compresses as it flies.
  • Ore drifts free. A pop's overflow scatters beside the rock for anyone to sweep up, in as many trips as it takes.
  • A living core. Cores are blue now, swelling as the rock is stripped, running orange as they overheat, and cracking open with the mining they take.
  • Thicker rocks deeper. Asteroids vary from thin-shelled pebbles to many-layered monsters, heavier with bedrock the farther out you fly.
  • Ship tutorials. An optional walkthrough for each ship, advancing as you actually do each step.
  • Cleared away. Chat, parties, fuel, money and the Fueler are gone for now — mine, haul and bank ore.
  • Crack, Bank and Haul. Mining was rebuilt from the ground up: asteroids are now hidden masonry you survey and cut, chunk by chunk, with ore banking into a glowing core that pays out when it pops.
  • Scanning matters. A rock reads as one solid mass until a scanner maps it: densities, ore veins and bedrock walls, visible to everyone working it.
  • Chain breaks. Breaking a chunk shocks the chunks around it, so breaks cascade and the order you cut in matters.
  • Ore is cargo now. Pop payouts land in miners' holds and overflow into cans and party crates, and ore only becomes credits when it's flown home.
  • Parties share everything. A party splits all its ore evenly on delivery, wherever its members are.
  • One ship per role. Miner, Scanner, Booster, Fueler and Hauler are ships of their own, swapped at the station.
  • Support for hire. Support ships can post a charge, and anyone outside their party can hire them on the spot.
  • Every ship mines. Support ships carry a light laser of their own; miners remain the specialists.
  • Bigger asteroids. Rocks grew and now run deeper, layered differently by size.
  • Drop cargo. Ships can jettison their hold for a hauler to collect — haulers themselves still never drop.
  • The core driller. The Scanner now surveys by drilling: anchor to a rock and bore a revealing line straight toward its core — but grinding through bedrock puts the ore around it at risk.
  • Rich bedrock. Bedrock now hides ore of its own, and only a boosted laser can cut it cleanly.
  • Ring 3. A third, farther ring with the biggest rocks, the rarest ores, and bedrock everywhere — some of its cores are sealed in it.
  • Plays on phones. Landscape touch controls: twin sticks to fly and fire, tap to pick up, hold for menus, pinch to zoom, with a compact interface and chat tucked behind a bubble.
  • Heat. Mining runs chunks orange and the heat pours into the core when they break — a hot core cracks faster, but push it too far and it blows early, taking the rest of the rock with it.
  • The Booster's pulse and coolant. Boosters now boost everyone nearby at the press of a button — boosted ships glow blue — and can switch to a coolant beam that cools overworked rock.
  • The drill, hands-on. The Scanner's drill is a held beam now: keep it on and circle the rock to sweep column after column.
  • Paid in ore. Delivery splits the ore itself between everyone tagged on it, rounded up in each player's favor, into station storage — selling is each player's own choice.
  • Claims and marks. Towing a rock claims it for you and your party, and rocks can be marked to stay on your party's minimap.
Oct 17, 2026
  • The Shop. A new station window to buy modules and hulls, with what each one does.
  • Upgrades. Every module can be upgraded, and bigger hulls fit more modules at once.
  • Ships show their modules. Each module you fit adds its own section to your ship.
  • Cargo you can see. Your hold and station storage now show every unit, and you can check what it's worth and who helped mine it.
  • Claiming ore. Broken asteroids leave their ore beside them for anyone to claim.
  • New ore names. Ores are now named Alpha through Epsilon, rarer down the alphabet.
  • Ship menus and trading. Right-click a ship to invite, whisper, fuel or refine it, or to trade your cargo to a hauler.
  • All sectors open. The whole ring system is open all the time.
  • Party and corporation invites. Invites now pop up to accept or decline, and leaders and owners can remove members.
  • Friends on the minimap. Your party and corporation show on the minimap wherever they are.
  • Field refining. Refinery ships now offer to refine the cargo of everyone nearby.
  • Tutorial. New pilots get a short guided tour of mining, delivering, refining, selling and upgrading.
  • Fog of war. Ships now see only their surroundings, so the rings hold surprises again.
  • Wider rings. Both asteroid rings grew to the same width, with more room to mine.
  • More alive. Engines leave trails, mining throws sparks, asteroids crack apart with a rumble, claimed ore flies into your hold, and the whole interface moves with you.
Oct 16, 2026
  • Saturn's rings. A new 2D world: fly out from the station around Saturn to mine the asteroids in its rings, with richer ores deeper out.
  • Mining together. Everyone who helps break an asteroid gets their name on the ore, and every named player gets a copy when it's delivered.
  • Modules. Fit your ship as a miner, hauler, fueler, tractor, command ship, refinery or scanner.
  • The station. Sell ore, refine it, turn ice into fuel, refuel and buy modules.
  • Fuel. Ships run on fuel, and fuelers can top up anyone who runs dry. Stranded ships can call for help.
  • Parties and corporations. Team up with other players, with their own chat channels.
  • Shards. Crowded zones split into copies so there's room for everyone, and parties stay together.
