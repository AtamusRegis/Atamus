The Expanse

Atamus is a multiplayer space mining game you play right in your browser.

Start with a single mining frigate and a station in your own corner of the Expanse. Lock onto asteroids, cut ore, sell it on the market, and train your pilots in new licenses.

Buy bigger hulls, fit them with lasers and modules, and grow from one ship into a mining fleet. Push your ships past their limits when it counts.

Updates

Oct 10, 2026

Drones and propulsion

Launch mining drones and press F to send them at your target. Afterburners and microwarpdrives take over from warping inside a belt or around the station, and cargohold expansions and mining laser upgrades boost your fit.

Oct 9, 2026

The Expanse

Everyone now shares one home system: four planets, the Expanse Station and six stargates around a blue sun. Zoom out to see the whole system, then right-click (or hold) a planet, the station or a belt to warp there. Asteroid belts appear as more pilots come online, and they don't last forever.

Oct 9, 2026

Asteroid instances

Belts are gone. Your system now has asteroid beacons that link to shared asteroid fields for up to five players. When a beacon glows, fly over and jump. Your ships keep mining while you're offline, and fresh jettison cans stay yours for 30 minutes.

Oct 9, 2026

Warp windows and hull bonuses

Warping now opens a glowing window ahead of your ship. You cross as a ball of light and drop out of a second window right at your destination. Hulls now boost the modules they're built for, and every ship has an acceleration stat.

Oct 9, 2026

Music, settings and hotkeys

The Expanse has a soundtrack, with volume controls in the new Settings window. Fire your modules with the number keys, and pick a pilot from the fleet panel.

Oct 9, 2026

Fitting and running hot

Ships now have hardpoints, disposition and a capacitor. Buy modules on the market and fit them in your station. Run more modules than your capacitor can handle, but watch the heat.

Oct 9, 2026

A fresh start

Every pilot has been reset so everyone starts on equal footing. Training is quick for now while we test.

Oct 8, 2026

Station market and pilots

Buy ships and training manuals from the station market. Each account can now have up to three pilots.

Oct 8, 2026

Mining in the Expanse

Mining lasers, the auto miner, station hangars and jettison cans. Mine ore, haul it home and sell it.
