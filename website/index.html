Updates

Oct 31, 2026
  • A seaport. Atamus starts over as a single-player seaport: boats dock at your berths loaded with colorful containers.
  • Forklifts. Tell your forklifts which container goes where, and keep a way clear for them to get there.
  • Storage areas. Walled storage areas in the yard to keep your containers in.
  • Trucks. Trucks pull up wanting a container in their color; bring them the right one and they leave happy.
