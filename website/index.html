Atamus

WIP prototype of a cargo station management game in space.

Play now

Updates

Nov 8, 2026
  • New game: Pet Delivery Co. Ride your pet around town delivering mail with friends, hatch eggs for new pets and train them to deliver faster.
  • Pet Delivery Co.: flying pets, dragons, butterflies and dinosaurs, floating islands to deliver to, hills, roaming animal control trucks, shorter contracts, and confetti and fireworks to celebrate.
Nov 6, 2026
  • Build storage piece by piece. Storage slots, cranes, hauler spots and truck spots are now separate buildings you place wherever you like, and you pick each slot's shape as you build it.
  • Move and remove. Pick up and move what you've built, including whole platforms, or clear away storage you don't need.
  • Waiting lines. Add hauler spots to a loader arm's line so haulers wait their turn right there.
  • Folding cranes. Cranes now reach up and over, and fold away when they're not working.
  • Cranes work together. Cranes pass containers to each other to get them where they need to go.
  • Tidier stacks. Cranes keep their stacks flat when they have a moment to spare.
Nov 5, 2026
  • Into space. The port is now a cargo station in orbit: freighters dock, haulers fly freely between loader arms and storage platforms, and the containers are hexagonal, stacked in honeycombs.
  • Build your station. Place storage platforms, loader arms, customer pads and hauler docks where you want them, then grow your platforms and give them more robot arms.
  • Container shapes. Containers come as circles, squares and hexagons, and you choose the shape each storage slot holds. Every customer now comes for a container of its own color.
  • Robot arms. Storage platforms are now worked by robot arms, with deliveries coming in on one side and pickups going out on the other, and you can see the cargo riding on every ship.
  • Liftoff. Freighters fire their engines as they dock, and new storage platforms and hauler docks fly up into place when you buy them.
Nov 3, 2026
  • The port in 3D. The game is now in 3D, with a camera you can tilt, turn and zoom.
Nov 1, 2026
  • One-tap controls. Everything now works with single taps, and the game is much easier to play on a phone.
  • A waiting line for the cranes. Tractors now line up along the quay road when the cranes are busy, so you can keep dozens on the go.
  • New roads. Customers with their container waiting drive straight in from the east gate, the rest park first, and everyone leaves by a new exit lane out the west gate. Every storage area now has its own street up to the quay.
  • Everyday cargo. Gray, white and black containers are now everyday cargo for the grey trucks, while colored trucks come for their own color.
  • A livelier port. Cranes now swing and hoist with a sense of weight, and the cranes, trucks and ships got a lot more detail.
  • Exports. Export trucks now bring containers to a new export area, and your tractors load them onto the ships. Tractors also deliver by themselves once loaded, and the lot has room for more customers.
  • Auto tractors and more cranes. Buy tractors that run on their own or ones you drive yourself, and grow the quay to four cranes, each with its own lines.
  • Ships of every size, on the clock. Small, medium and large ships now arrive, and each one only waits so long before it sails.
  • Tractor parking. New and idle tractors now wait in their own parking area beside the dock.
  • Follow the container. A line now leads from any customer truck to its container.
  • Two-lane roads. Every road has a passing lane, and the roads were laid out so trucks head straight where they need to go.
  • Every customer wants one container. Select a truck to see its container flash, and tell the cranes which ship slot to unload.
  • Grow your port. Start small, earn from every customer you serve, and buy more tractors, cranes, storage and parking.
Oct 31, 2026
  • A container terminal. Atamus starts over as a single-player container port: one big container ship at a time, unloaded by two cranes.
  • Storage yards. Tall storage areas where cranes stack containers three high.
  • Traffic. A bigger port with one-way roads where trucks queue up and wait their turn, lines under the ship cranes and parking bays beside the storage cranes.
  • Trucks. Send your yard trucks between the ship cranes and the storage, and send customer trucks to collect the container in their color.
