# Atamus: how the game works today

This is a living spec of the game. Read it before every patch, and update it in the same commit as any change to behavior. Rules marked **(owner)** were decided explicitly by the owner, so keep them unless the owner says otherwise.

## The ring game (owner, 2026-10-16)

The owner restarted the design as a **cooperative gather-and-craft MMO, like Palia, with no PvP**, and asked for a **2D** prototype: "armed with the gdd art and server stuff, go make the prototype". The design document is the artifact **"Atamus Ring Mining"** (draft 6, https://claude.ai/artifact/CfeRmvahLSwVXTSod92caB) and the ship art is **"Atamus ships"** (https://claude.ai/artifact/LMCjSamVAaYs8WbwALuzJw); `docs/DESIGN.md` summarizes them. **The 3D team game it replaces is on the `legacy-3d-v2` branch** (tip `23df787`); the first 3D version is on `legacy-3d-v1` and the 2D space game on `legacy-2d`. Nothing of the 3D game's background, stars or grid was reused (owner). Carried over: accounts, chat (rebuilt for the new channels), the camera following the ship with zoom, the admin and moderator tools, live updates. No Build menu.

**Old data is kept (owner):** every old table (the 3D game's `world3d*`, the 2D game's) stays untouched. The ring game uses `ring_players` and `ring_corps`.

Every number is in `packages/server/src/game/rules.js` (the design document's "first numbers", meant to change once we play) and is sent to the client in the hello.

## The world

- **A 2D plane, north up.** Saturn sits at the center (nothing flies inside it); **one station** sits just north of it. Around them, **two asteroid rings**: ring 1 (3–6 km out) and ring 2 (9–14 km out). **Deeper means outward**: ring 2 has the rarer ores and bigger rocks. A **red dashed locked line** at 15 km: nobody flies past it.
- **Four sectors, North, East, South and West**, radiate from Saturn (owner). **One sector is open per 50 players online** (North first, then East, South, West); around Saturn and the station (2.6 km) everything is open. A sector that's no longer needed **waits 10 minutes before closing**, and a ship already inside a closing or closed sector can stay and fly out, but nobody new enters. Closed sectors are shaded on the map and minimap.
- **The zone** shown top center: "Station", or the ring and sector ("Ring 1 · North"), plus the shard while shards are running ("Ring 1 · North · Shard 2").

## Shards (owner: "crowded zones split into copies once all four sectors are open")

- **Only once all four sectors are open** (and none is closing), **each zone holds 40 players per copy**. A ship picks its copy whenever it enters a zone (or logs in): **a party member's copy of that zone if one is there**, else the copy it's already in while that has room, else the first copy with room, else a new one. **Accepting a party invite moves you into a party member's copy** if they're in your zone. With shards off, ships go back to copy 1 as they change zones.
- **Each copy has its own asteroids, loose ore, ships, effects and distress beacons**; you can only see, mine, scan, tow, pump, refine and hand ore within your copy. **Zone chat stays in your copy**; World, Party, Corp and Whispers reach every copy. Storage, credits, parties and corporations are shared. Changing copy drops your links to other ships (pumping, refining); a towed asteroid comes with you. Staff teleporting to a player join that player's copy.

## Asteroids

- **15 sizes; size n has n cracks** (owner: "15 asteroid sizes for 15 variations of crack counts"). Each crack has health; **mining is holding the laser on the rock**. Ring 1 has sizes 1–10, ring 2 sizes 4–15; smaller sizes are commoner. Bigger rocks are bigger on screen.
- **Each crack that breaks drops one ore unit**, a small square that drifts out toward the ship lasering it. When the last crack breaks the rock is gone.
- **The ore is hidden until scanned.** Ring 1: iron, nickel, ice. Ring 2: titanium, cobalt, ice, platinum, a little iron.
- **Seeded and changing (owner):** the rings are cut into 400 m cells; each rock's seed comes from a secret only the server knows (with the 6-hour epoch, the shard, the cell, the slot and how many times the slot has rerolled), so nobody can map the field ahead. **The client builds the rocks itself (owner, 2026-10-16):** each untouched rock near you arrives as just its seed, and the client turns it into the same position and size as the server (`ringgen.js`, the same file on both). Rocks someone has worked on (lasered, scanned, towed) are sent in full. **The ore never comes from the seed**: it stays on the server until the rock is scanned. **A mined-out rock comes back as a new rock (different ore and size) after 3 minutes**; a rock nobody touches for 30 minutes despawns and is replaced the same way; the whole field reshuffles every 6 hours. Cells only exist near players and are dropped when nobody's been near for 2 minutes (keeping rocks someone has worked on). Each player is sent only what's within 3.2 km.

## Ships and controls

- **One hull for everyone** (owner), with a **free base laser**, a **5-unit hold**, a **100-unit fuel tank** and **one module**. New players start with 200 credits, a full tank and the **Miner** module.
- **W / S** thrust forward / back along the nose, **A / D** turn, **Q / E** slide sideways. **The mouse aims; holding the left button fires the laser** at the first rock along the aim (170 m reach). **Right-click** uses your module on the ship or rock under the pointer. **F** switches an active module on or off. **The mouse wheel zooms** (kept per browser). The camera follows your ship.
- **Fuel:** flying at full thrust burns fuel, and active modules burn it while working. **An empty tank means crawling** at a fifth of the speed, and the ship sends a **distress beacon: a red "!" every player in its shard sees** (above its name; at the screen edge when it's off screen, and on the minimap) until it has fuel again.
- Ships bump off asteroids and pass through each other. Ships can't be destroyed.

## Modules (owner: one at a time, swapped only at the station)

| Module | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Miner** | F switches on the big laser: 4x mining speed, burning fuel while it fires. |
| **Hauler** | A 40-unit hold. Only a hauler can take another ship's ore. |
| **Fueler** | A 700-unit tank. Right-click a ship to pump fuel into it (until full or you stop); **the world pays the fueler** 2.5 credits per unit pumped, not the ship being fueled. |
| **Tractor** | Right-click a rock to tow it behind you on a leash (slower the bigger it is; burns fuel by size). A rock can't be moved once its first crack has broken. |
| **Command** | F switches on a boost: every ship mining within 300 m mines 1.5x faster (several command ships don't stack), burning fuel. |
| **Scanner** | Right-click a rock (800 m reach) to reveal its ore to everyone. |
| **Refinery** | Refines raw ore in the field, one unit every 15 s for 2 fuel: F refines your own hold, right-click a ship to refine theirs. |

The station sells each module once (Miner 500, Hauler 800, Fueler 800, Command 1,200, Tractor 1,000, Scanner 1,000, Refinery 1,500 credits); owned modules are equipped there for free. Each tank keeps its fuel; swapping to a module with a smaller tank turns the extra into fuel blocks in storage.

## Ore, names and delivery (owner: "everyone gets paid out the full amount for helping")

- **Every ore unit carries the names of everyone who helped**, each at most once: every ship **lasering the rock at the moment the crack breaks**, every command ship that boosted them during that crack, whoever scanned or towed the rock, and **party fuelers whose fuel the miner is burning**.
- **Any ship that delivers it is named too** (and its party fuelers). **Delivering** (flying into the station's circle) puts **a full copy of each unit into the station storage of every named player**. Names stop at the station.
- **Loose ore** flies into the nearest ship within 50 m that has room. It despawns after 30 minutes untouched.
- **Custody (owner):** ore in a hold can't be dropped or given away, with **one exception: a non-hauler can hand its whole hold to a hauler** (right-click the hauler within 150 m); haulers can't hand ore on. **If a carrier logs off, after 30 minutes their hold "crawls home"** and is delivered after the crawl time (they aren't added as deliverer). **A banned player's hold is delivered** at once.
- **Refining:** a refined unit takes **a fifth of the space** and **sells for 1.5x**. Raw ore is brown, refined is purple, in the hold bar and on the ships' fill gauges. Field refining (Refinery) keeps the names. **The station refines for free, one unit a minute**, and keeps going while you're offline.

## The station

Flying into the station's circle docks the ship (delivering its hold) and opens the **Station panel** on the right: **Storage** (each ore, raw and refined, with **Sell** and **Refine**; ice also has **Make fuel**; fuel blocks have **Fill tank**), the **Queue** (refining in purple and fuel blocks in blue, with progress), **Fuel** (**Fill** the tank at 2 credits a unit), and **Modules** (Buy / Equip / Equipped). **Make fuel** turns ice into fuel blocks (25 fuel each, 15 s a block): cheaper than buying fuel, since ice sells for 30. No trading between players for now; progression is modules (and hulls later).

## Parties and corporations

- **Parties of up to 10** (owner): right-click a name → Invite to party, or `/party invite Name` (`/invite Name`); `/party accept`, `/party leave`. The party list shows at the top left. A fueler's name rides on fuel only within a party, and leaving takes it off for good.
- **Corporations**, any size: `/corp create Name`, `/corp invite Name` (or from a name), `/corp accept`, `/corp leave`.

## Chat (rebuilt 2026-10-16)

- **At the bottom right**: tabs **All | World | Zone | Party | Corp | Whisper** (which lines show), the lines, then the input with its label **"[Channel] Name:"**. **The tab is what you read; the channel is where you type**: picking a tab points the channel at it, and **TAB while typing cycles the channel** (World → Zone → Party → Corp → Whisper, skipping ones you're not in).
- **Zone** reaches players in your zone (and shard); **Party** and **Corp** your party and corporation; **Whispers** one player (`/w Name message`, or `/w "Name with spaces" message`). No one online by that name: "<name> is offline."
- **Enter** opens the input and sends; Escape or an empty line closes it. Game keys are ignored while typing.
- **Right-clicking a name in chat**: **Whisper**, **Invite to party**, **Invite to corporation** (when you're in one), **Report** (a short note to the admins with the player's recent lines; at most 5 in 10 minutes), and for staff **Admin** (the disciplinary panel). A muted player's lines go nowhere. 240 characters at most. No history.

## Map and HUD

- **The minimap** (bottom left): Saturn, the station, the rings, the locked line, closed sectors, your ship and distress beacons; the wheel zooms it.
- **The HUD** (bottom center, between the minimap and the chat; in windows too narrow for that, above them): the module (and whether it's on), the hold bar (brown raw, purple refined), the fuel bar, credits.
- Names show over other ships.

## Accounts and sessions

Kept from before (`auth.js`, `sessions.js`, `security.js`): sign-up with the EULA, log in / log out, Remember Password, **password recovery with the three security questions only**, device ids, failed-login backoff (per account and device, in memory), bans (told at login, with ban appeals written from the login page) and the session cookie. **No login records are kept** (owner, 2026-10-13). An account locked in the 2D era stays locked until its recovery questions unlock it. **One session per account:** a new tab or device replaces the old one (close 4002: "Playing on another tab or device"). A dead session (4001) goes back to the login page.
- **One account per device** (owner, kept 2026-10-13): each browser has a device id; while one account is in the game on it, logging in as another account is refused ("Another account is playing on this device."), and a second account's game connection from that device is closed (4003) with the same message.

- **The website** (atamus.io) is unchanged except play.html: **no more character or pilot creation** — a logged-in account goes straight to **Enter System** (with the **Reset location** checkbox under it, which puts your ship back at the station on entering; everything on the Welcome back card is centered, owner 2026-10-13). **The front page (owner, 2026-10-14):** its description is just "WIP prototype of a cooperative space mining game." with Play now (owner, 2026-10-16); the "Help build Atamus" panel is gone; the Updates list holds only the current game's entries (cleared again for the ring game, 2026-10-16). Login, signup, recovery and the EULA are as they were. The pixel logo and icons stay.
- **The admin panel** (owner, 2026-10-13): staff accounts (role head / admin / mod; Nyssary is the Head Admin) see an **[A] button** (on the right edge of the screen, centered top to bottom; moved left of the station panel while it's open) (owner, 2026-10-13; hover: Admin); **being logged in as a staff account is enough, no second admin password**. **One window, one size for every section** (owner, 2026-10-13: the Players size — the tabs, the search box and 6 rows), so switching sections doesn't flicker (the new section is drawn off-screen and swapped in when its data arrives); its sections are **floating icon buttons on its left** (names on hover): **Players**, **Reports**, **Appeals**. **Players:** **Online / Offline** tabs, then a **search box** for the selected list (any part of a name), then the list: **6 rows show, then it scrolls** while the tabs and search stay at the top. A row: **name** (up to 20 characters, monospace) | **Mute** tag | **Ban** tag | **last played** ("now", or how long ago; from the 3D world's saved positions) | **Discipline** (the disciplinary panel). **Both tags are on every row** (owner, 2026-10-13): **grayed** unless running; **lit while running — Mute yellow, Ban red — with the time left inside, under the word** (∞ for indefinite); grayed, they hold **the total time the player has spent muted / banned** inside (a lifted one counts only until it was lifted; nothing if never; kept in a `discipline` table from 2026-10-13, earlier ones aren't counted). Kicks aren't shown. Offline lists everyone else, most recently played first (200 at most). **Reports:** players' reports (from a name in chat), open first; click one for the note and the reported player's recent lines, with **Discipline** and **Close**. **Appeals:** the ban appeals; open one to read it, reply, and Accept (unbans) or Deny, with an optional note. Admins appear to players as "Admin". Every action goes in the admin log. Hidden on touch. (`admin.js`, `website/adminpanel.js`.)
- **Moderators (owner, 2026-10-14):** **Nyssary (head) makes and removes moderators** from the disciplinary panel (**Make moderator** on a player, **Remove moderator** on a moderator; only she can, and not on other staff). **A moderator can Kick, Mute and Ban** (nothing else: no kill / respawn / teleports, no unmute / unban) and sees the **Players** and **Reports** sections only (no Appeals). Their **mutes and bans of up to 3 days apply at once; 7 days and Indefinite are requests** (kept in the `approvals` table): the moderator sees "Sent to Nyssary for approval." and **Nyssary approves or denies them in an Approvals section only she has** (open ones first, with Approve / Deny; decided ones show who decided). An approved one runs from when it's approved. The server enforces all of it; a new moderator's [A] button appears without reconnecting. Every step goes in the admin log (requested, approved, denied, made / removed moderator). **Staff badges in chat (owner, 2026-10-14):** left of a staff member's name in chat, a small circle: **blue with a white "m"** for a moderator, **red with a white "A"** for an admin (Nyssary and admins); hover shows Moderator / Admin; the same badge shows by their name in the admin panel's player list and on the disciplinary panel (owner, 2026-10-14). The server tags each line with the sender's current role (a role change shows on their next line).
- **The disciplinary panel** (owner, 2026-10-13: its own window, no reason text boxes), from Discipline or the **Admin** item on a name in chat: **Type** — Kick, Mute, Ban, Kill, Respawn, Teleport to me, Teleport to player, plus **Unmute** / **Unban** when the player is muted / banned; **Reason** (mute: Language, Spam; ban: Language, Spam, Disruptive Behavior); a **Length** slider for mutes and bans with notches **5 min, 30 min, 6 h, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, Indefinite**; **Add note** (an optional note to the player); **Cancel / Accept**. What the player sees: kicked — "Kicked" (": note") and back to the Welcome back page; banned — "Banned: reason — note", then Log in shows the ban and its appeal; muted — a chat line "Muted <length>: reason — note", and their lines go nowhere; killed or respawned — back at the station, stopped (the two are the same: ships can't be destroyed); teleported — stopped, 150 m from the other ship. For the other types a note shows as a line in their chat. Staff can't discipline themselves or other staff (teleports work on anyone but yourself); teleports need both ships in the world.

## Live updates (kept)

- Every push to `main` warns every connected player ahead of the switch — **1 s for now (owner, 2026-10-12: "make countdown 1 second for now"**; it was 30 s and goes back up when the owner says): the game shows **"Update incoming — N s"** top center, then sends them to `play.html?updating=1`, which waits for the new build and offers Enter System again (owner, 2026-10-14: **not a timer** — the countdown carries the commit going live, and the page shows Enter System the moment the server and website report that version (or a newer one) and the server answers a login check; every waiting page checks once a second, so it comes back for everyone at once. Each check gives up after 4 s so a restarting server can't hang it, a reload continues the same wait, and only a deploy that never lands is let through after 5 minutes). **A game page that's already the new website version ignores the server's later "website switched" notice** (owner, 2026-10-14: the waiting page can see the new website before the server does, and players who had just come back in were sent out to wait again). The length lives in `gamehttp.js` (`announceUpdate(part, 1)`) and the `sleep` in both deploy workflows.
- **One trip to the update page (owner, 2026-10-15):** a push that changes both the server and the website says so in its countdown (each deploy workflow reads the pushed files), so players wait on the update page until **both** are live, instead of being let in after the first and sent out again for the second. The update page also waits until the new game page itself is being served and the server says nothing is still on its way. **While an update is in progress** (from the countdown until every changed half is live; at most 5 minutes; the server keeps it in `uploads/update.json`, so a restart mid-update still knows), **nobody gets in:** the Welcome back page and the game send anyone (signing in, opening the game, connecting) straight to the update page, which lets everyone in together once it's done.
- The deploy workflows are unchanged (`deploy-website.yml` on `website/**`, `deploy-server.yml` on `packages/server/**`).
