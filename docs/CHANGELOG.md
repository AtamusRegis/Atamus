## 2026-10-14

- **Manual reload and panel tweaks (owner):** reloading now needs R (an empty magazine stops firing until you press it). In the Weapon panel a new weapon's numbers start at 0, and the panel stays open after CHANGE / CREATE.
- **Draggable Weapon panel (owner):** the Weapon panel can be dragged by its top bar.
- **Weapon panel (owner):** staff can open a Weapon panel (Z) to tune or create weapons: optimal range and falloff, bullet lifetime and speed, shot rate, damage, spread (default, per shot, recovery), aim line rotation speed, magazine and reload. The Railgun is the first weapon; saving equips it on your ship. Shots now follow the aim line, which turns toward the pointer at the weapon's rotation speed, and run out after a magazine until reloaded.
- **Gentler spread (owner):** each railgun shot now widens the aim cone by 1.5° each way (was 3°).
- **Railgun fire rate and spread (owner):** the railgun fires every half second now, but every shot widens your aim cone by 3° each way (it narrows back 3° a second), and shots land anywhere inside it, so rapid fire gets inaccurate. The aim line shows the cone.
- **Smooth routes (owner):** test ship routes have rounded corners, drawn and flown as curves, so ships sweep through them at speed instead of stopping to turn at each point.
- **Test ship AI and route editing (owner):** test ships now respect clouds (they can't see a player deep in a nebula or through its core) along with every other line-of-sight blocker. A handle in the middle of a route moves the whole path; editing or moving a path no longer snaps the ship back to spawn: it flies back to its path, shown by a white dotted leash. Route dashes are animated toward the next point.
- **Test ship routes and attacks (owner):** placed test ships respawn like players. Selecting one in the Build menu shows Add route and Attack player buttons above it: a route is an editable loop starting and ending at its spawn (marked S) that it flies; with Attack player on it shoots players it can see within 400 m, and a route ship chases them up to 400 m from where it left its route before going back.
- **Snappier impacts (owner):** impact sparks and debris burn out about twice as fast, and black soot particles now fly among them.
- **Directional sparks (owner):** collision and railgun sparks now burst from the exact impact point and spray in a cone back toward where the hit came from.
- **Enter System returns when the update is live (owner):** the update page now waits for the exact new version on the server and website and for the server to accept logins, then brings Enter System back for everyone at once (checked every second), instead of relying on a timer.
- **Staff badges (owner):** the letters now sit centered in their circles, and the badges also show in the admin panel's player list and on the disciplinary panel.

- **Staff badges in chat (owner):** moderators get a blue circle with a white m left of their name in chat, admins a red circle with an A.
- **Moderators (owner):** Nyssary can make and remove moderators from the disciplinary panel. Moderators can kick, mute and ban (up to 3 days at once); their 7-day and indefinite mutes and bans go to Nyssary, who approves or denies them in a new Approvals section. Moderators see only Players and Reports, and don't get the Build menu.
- **Background at half again (owner):** the colored space is now drawn at a sixth of the screen's resolution (was a third).
- **Materializing respawns (owner):** a respawned ship now appears as a hologram revealed from nose to tail, then solidifies; it can't move, fire or be damaged until it's solid.
- **Second rendering pass (owner, aiming for 60 fps):** the colored space and the clouds are drawn at a third of the resolution and stretched (they're soft, so it doesn't show), the shadows are now flat 50% black shapes drawn straight on the ground (no shadow texture), the resolution adapts automatically when frames run slow, and MSAA is skipped on high-DPI screens. In testing, frame rates roughly doubled to tripled.
- **Reverse steering (owner):** while backing up, A and D swap, so backing up with A swings the ship back toward its left, like a car in reverse.
- **Front page (owner):** today's Updates entry brought up to date (railgun slugs, clouds blocking sight, mutual line of sight, the new flight controls); the stop marker dropped from Oct 13's entry since it's gone.
- **Faster clouds (owner: optimization pass):** nebula clouds are drawn at half resolution and laid over the scene (they look the same, at about half the drawing cost), and their edge puffs stop updating once they've settled with no moving ship nearby.
- **Railgun projectiles (owner):** the railgun now fires a fast glowing slug instead of an instant beam. A ship the shooter can't see past walls and structures isn't hit: the slug flies straight through it.
- **Railgun (owner):** left click fires an instant railgun shot toward the pointer (once a second, 1,000 m); it's stopped by blocks, Build pieces and ships, and a ship hit takes 25 damage. Everyone nearby sees the beam.

Ships fly through them; inside, a ship fades the deeper it goes (25% at 150 m in), and a ship sitting still is invisible unless you're in the same cloud within 100 m.

- **Slower turning (owner):** A / D turn at 0.5 rad/s (was 2).
- **Ship-relative controls (owner):** W flies the way the ship faces, S flies it backward (slower), A and D turn it. Sprint works only flying forward, and now also while aiming.
- **Reticle independent of the ship (owner):** aiming no longer turns the ship toward the reticle or slows strafing; the ship always faces and moves the way WASD pushes it.

- **Impact sparks (owner):** ship collisions throw out debris and fire sparks at the point of impact, more for harder hits.

Ships have health; a health bar shows over damaged ships, and a ship at 0 comes back at full health near the center.
- **Drift (owner):** a ship's motion now swings toward a new direction more slowly than the nose turns (3.5 rad/s, was 8), so ships drift slightly through turns; the nose's turning eases a little more. **Fix: hidden ships no longer stop the aim line** (it could reveal them before they showed).
- **Smoother ship movement (owner):** turning spins up and eases in instead of starting and stopping dead, ships lean into turns, corrections from the server blend in smoothly, and other ships glide along curves between updates. Speeds and handling are unchanged.
- **Smoother visibility (owner):** ships fade smoothly from hidden at 25% visible to solid at 75% (no steps), appear fast and disappear slowly, the pivot line now counts 20% (was half), and visibility updates 30 times a second.
- **Editor visibility lines (owner):** only other ships' lines to your pivot (and the pivot-to-pivot line) are drawn, not yours to them (the percentage still counts both).
- **Aim Bounds and Visibility (owner):** the editor's Sight tab is now Aim Bounds (the hitbox for aiming and shooting); a new Visibility tab places free points that decide how visible a ship is. Visibility now weighs the pivot-to-pivot line as half and the Visibility points' lines as the other half.

- **Visibility tuning (owner):** every side of a ship's outline now adds a point at its middle; steps are 75%+ solid, 50-75% half, 35-50% a quarter, under 35% hidden.
